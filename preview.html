About Mehsana Urban Transport

Mehsana Urban Transport is the premier public transportation service serving Mehsana city and surrounding areas. We provide reliable bus services connecting all major locations including Modhera, Vijapur, and industrial areas.

Our mission is to make city travel convenient, affordable, and eco-friendly for students, workers, and families. With real-time tracking, digital ticketing, and 24/7 customer support, we ensure hassle-free commuting for everyone.
